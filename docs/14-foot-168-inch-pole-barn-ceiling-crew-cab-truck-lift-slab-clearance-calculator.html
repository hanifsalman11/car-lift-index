Frequently Asked Questions (14-Foot (168-Inch) Pole Barn Ceiling Crew-Cab Truck Lift)

How tall does my garage ceiling need to be for 14-Foot (168-Inch) Pole Barn Ceiling Crew-Cab Truck Lift?

14-Foot (168-Inch) Pole Barn Ceiling Crew-Cab Truck Lift requires at least 168 inches (14.0 feet) of clear ceiling height. For double-stacking two vehicles on a 4-post lift, add the height of Vehicle 1 + Vehicle 2 + 12 inches (for runways and lock disengagement clearance)—meaning a 168" ceiling fits two cars whose combined height is up to 156 inches.

How thick does concrete need to be for a 12,000 lb car lift?

Per ACI 318 and lift manufacturer engineering specs, a 12,000-lb lift requires a minimum of 6 inches of 3,500 PSI concrete (cured for at least 28 days) with 3/4" × 5-1/2" Wedge Anchors torqued to 110 ft-lbs. If your garage slab is thin (<4") or cracked, cut and pour two 4'×4'×12" reinforced 3,500 PSI concrete footing pads.

What size 240V breaker, wire, and hydraulic oil do I need for 14-Foot (168-Inch) Pole Barn Ceiling Crew-Cab Truck Lift?

A standard 2.5 HP to 3.0 HP 240V single-phase car lift hydraulic power unit draws ~18–22 Amps under full load (with high startup inrush), requiring a dedicated 30-Amp 2-pole circuit breaker wired with 10/2 AWG Copper + NEMA L6-30 Plug (240V), and 16 quarts (3.5–4.0 gallons) of AW-32 or AW-46 anti-wear hydraulic oil.
